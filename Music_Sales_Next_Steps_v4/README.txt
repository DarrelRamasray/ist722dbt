Music Sales - guide v4
======================

1. Read Music_Sales_Next_Steps_Guide.pdf.
2. The music_sales folder is a complete dbt project plus every Music Sales
   script. Part 4 of the guide uploads that whole folder to the
   ist722dbt repository, next to demo, northwind and quickstart.

Copy scripts from the files, not from the PDF (copying from a PDF can
join every line into one).

Never upload your private key file or the CSV files to GitHub.
